## Marathon Vandal for Calico 1.3

- New look: Null Tempest, her PlayStation promo outfit, with a blue arm cannon to match. Its sleeves swing like the Default look.
- The Default look is the same as in 1.2.

### Pick one look
- **Vandal_Calico.zip**: Default. Her standard Marathon outfit, with blue lips and a pink arm cannon.
- **Vandal_NullTempest_Calico.zip**: Null Tempest. Her PlayStation promo outfit in grey and blue, with a face mask and a blue arm cannon.

Each zip is a separate mod. Enable only one.

### Install
1. Download the zip for the look you want. Keep it zipped.
2. In Deadlock Mod Manager, click **Add Local Mod**, drop the zip in and click **Add**.
3. In **Mods Library**, click **Enable**, then start the game with **Launch modded**.

Full instructions: https://vyrena.github.io/marathon-vandal-calico/
