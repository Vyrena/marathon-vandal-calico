## Marathon Vandal for Calico 1.2

- The arm cannon now keeps its shape in every animation, including reloading.
- Fixed pieces of the arm flashing across the screen when you start or stop shooting.
- The cannon is now always out. The glove from 1.0 no longer swaps in out of combat.

### Install
1. Download **Vandal_Calico.zip** below. Keep it zipped.
2. In Deadlock Mod Manager, click **Add Local Mod**, drop the zip in and click **Add**.
3. In **Mods Library**, click **Enable**, then start the game with **Launch modded**.

Full instructions: https://vyrena.github.io/marathon-vandal-calico/
